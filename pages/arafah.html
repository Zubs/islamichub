Why This Day

This is the best day of the year

But why? Here are some virtues and blessings of the day!

مَا مِنْ يَوْمٍ أَكْثَرَ مِنْ أَنْ يُعْتِقَ اللَّهُ فِيهِ عَبْدًا مِنَ النَّارِ مِنْ يَوْمِ عَرَفَةَ
“There is no day on which Allah sets free more servants from the Fire than the Day of Arafah.”
The Prophet ﷺ · Narrated by 'Aishah (may Allah be pleased with her) · Sahih Muslim 1348
Action So ask Allah for forgiveness, and to be freed from the Hellfire.
🔥

Freed from the Fire

On no other day does Allah free more people from the Hellfire. Make istighfar the anchor of your du'ā.

📅

The Best Day for Du'ā

The Prophet ﷺ said the best du'ā is the du'ā of Arafah. From Dhuhr to Maghrib is your window — do not waste a moment.

✨

Deeds Are Multiplied

Allah swore by this day in the Qur'an (Al-Buruj 85:3). The ten days of Dhul Hijjah are the most beloved to Allah — this is their crown.

🔄

A New Beginning

Fasting on Arafah expiates the sins of the previous year and the coming year. (Sahih Muslim 1162)

“On this day, even as the Prophet ﷺ held his camel's rein with one hand, he would raise the other to the sky in du'ā — because he wanted to make the most of every moment of it.”
Du'ā made on this day can change everything.

لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ

Transliteration: Lā ilāha illallāh, waḥdahu lā sharīka lah, lahul-mulku wa lahul-ḥamdu, wa huwa ʿalā kulli shayʾin qadīr.

Translation: “There is no god but Allah, alone, with no partner. To Him belongs all sovereignty and all praise, and He has power over all things.”

Source: “The best thing I and the Prophets before me have said on the Day of Arafah.” — Tirmidhi 3585, Hasan.

The Prophet ﷺ spent the final hours of Arafah making du'ā with his hands raised. This is the time scholars describe as the golden hour. Pour out everything between Asr and Maghrib. Ask by Allah's names. Be specific. Repeat.

◔
The key window, Dhuhr to Maghrib. When it comes, do not hold back. Pour out everything.
A Way To Ask

Begin by calling on Allah through His beautiful names, then ask Him for whatever your heart longs for:

“Yā Allah, Al-Malik (The King), Al-Mālik (The Owner), Ar-Razzāq (The Giver of all provision), if anything were possible, I would ask You for...”

Before the day arrives

Know the weight

This is the day Allah completed the religion and perfected His favour (Qur'an 5:3). Approach it with seriousness.

Make tawbah now

Turn to Allah sincerely before the day. Clear your heart. Settle any wrongs between you and others.

Plan your du'ā list

Write down everything you want to ask. Be specific — your deen, your family, your health, the Ummah.

Intend to fast

If you are not performing Hajj, fast this day. It expiates two years of sins (Sahih Muslim 1162).

Remove distractions

Tell your people. Silence your phone. Give the day the respect it deserves.
